# PaperBuddy Product Notes

## Design Direction

PaperBuddy uses a premium fintech dashboard language: frosted surfaces, crisp contrast, teal and sky accents, restrained motion, dense finance modules, and operational tables.

## Core Flows

- Admin signs in by role and lands on the finance dashboard.
- Accountant searches a student, generates a fee quote, records payment, and issues a receipt.
- Parent checks due dates, pays with UPI QR, and downloads receipts.
- System predicts likely defaulters and queues reminder notices.
